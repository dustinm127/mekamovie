SYNOPSIS

A schizophrenic woman escapes her abuser and flees to an isolated rural home, where she is forced into a psychological crucible—battling the relentless voices in her head, the ghosts of her past, and a looming external presence that may or may not be real.

Blending elevated psychological horror, gaslighting tension, and visceral trauma-informed character study, MEKA delivers a relentless thrill ride with marquee crossover genre talent.

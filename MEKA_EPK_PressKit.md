## 2. LOGLINE & SYNOPSES

### Logline (1-Sentence Pitch):
> *"After escaping a violent abuser, a schizophrenic young woman seeks refuge in an isolated rural house, where the boundary between her inner demons and a sinister external presence begins to violently unravel."*

### Short Synopsis (50 Words):
> *"Fleeing an abusive past, a young woman living with schizophrenia isolates herself in an abandoned country house to rebuild her sanity. But as the haunting voices in her head intensify, an ominous presence stalks the property—forcing her into a terrifying psychological crucible where she cannot trust her own senses to survive."*

### Medium Synopsis (150 Words):
> *"Traumatized and desperate for sanctuary, Meka—a young woman diagnosed with severe schizophrenia—escapes her abusive partner and flees into the rural countryside, taking shelter in a secluded, empty home.*
>
> *Attempting to piece her shattered mind back together, she struggles to decipher reality from hallucination as disembodied voices, auditory horrors, and lingering childhood traumas echo through the halls. But her fragile sanctuary quickly collapses when signs of a living, malevolent intruder begin closing in on the property.*
>
> *Trapped between the psychological warfare inside her head and a looming external threat that grows more physical by the hour, she must wage war on two fronts—where determining what is real is the only thing standing between her survival and madness.*
>
> *Blending elevated psychological dread with visceral tension, MEKA delivers a relentless, trauma-informed thriller starring Tilda McSpadden, Billy Blair (Sin City, Alita: Battle Angel), Pam Dougherty (Suncoast), and Erica Muse (Chainsaw Man)."*

## 3. DIRECTOR’S STATEMENT (BY DUSTIN GRANT)

> *"Horror is at its most terrifying when the threat exists both outside your door and inside your own mind.*
>
> *With MEKA, I wanted to explore the visceral terror of schizophrenia and severe psychological trauma—not as a cinematic gimmick, but as an intimate, agonizing human reality. When you cannot trust your own eyes, ears, or memories, every shadow becomes an existential threat, and every quiet moment is deafening.*
>
> *As a filmmaker who wrote, directed, financed, and edited this story, my goal was to craft a claustrophobic, sensory-driven experience that puts the audience squarely inside our protagonist’s fractured perception. The house in MEKA is both a physical sanctuary and a mental prison. By combining grounded, emotionally raw performances from Tilda McSpadden, Billy Blair, Pam Dougherty, and Erica Muse with meticulous sound design and haunting cinematography, MEKA challenges the audience: what do you do when the voice warning you of danger might be the very thing trying to destroy you?*
>
> *MEKA is a testament to independent cinema—crafted with unyielding passion, uncompromising vision, and an authentic respect for psychological genre storytelling."*
>
> **— Dustin Grant, Writer / Director / Producer**

## 4. CAST BIOGRAPHIES & FILMOGRAPHIES

### **TILDA McSPADDEN** as *"Meka"* (Lead)
Tilda McSpadden delivers a powerhouse, emotionally raw breakout lead performance as Meka, a woman pushed to the absolute edge of sanity and physical survival. McSpadden embodies the harrowing dual reality of severe mental illness and primal fight-or-flight resilience with arresting nuance and vulnerability.

### **BILLY BLAIR** as *"Lead Antagonist / Co-Star"*
Billy Blair is a prolific Hollywood and independent film actor renowned for his intense, commanding screen presence in major studio blockbusters and cult genre favorites. Blair’s extensive filmography includes Robert Rodriguez and James Cameron’s **Alita: Battle Angel** (20th Century Fox), Robert Rodriguez and Frank Miller’s **Sin City: A Dame to Kill For**, Rob Zombie’s **3 from Hell**, **Machete Kills**, and **Jonah Hex** (Warner Bros). Blair brings a chilling, visceral gravity to *MEKA*.

### **PAM DOUGHERTY** as *"Supporting Principal"*
Pam Dougherty is a celebrated stage, screen, and voice veteran whose career spans four decades. Dougherty recently earned widespread acclaim for her featured role in the **Searchlight Pictures / Sundance Film Festival Official Selection *Suncoast***, starring alongside four-time Academy Award nominee Laura Linney, Woody Harrelson, and Nico Parker. Her live-action credits include Hollywood studio titles such as **Walking Tall**, **Dr. T and the Women**, **Universal Soldier: The Return**, and **Courage Under Fire**. Internationally, Dougherty is a revered voice icon across legendary anime franchises including **Fullmetal Alchemist**, **Dragon Ball Z**, **Yu Yu Hakusho**, and **One Piece**.

### **ERICA MUSE** as *"Supporting Principal"*
Erica Muse is a leading voice actress and screen talent recognized globally across major anime blockbusters, video game franchises, and live-action productions. Muse’s premier credits include the smash-hit global anime phenomena **Chainsaw Man**, **My Hero Academia**, **Spy × Family**, **Frieren: Beyond Journey's End**, and **Attack on Titan**. Muse brings a dynamic emotional resonance and a massive, highly engaged crossover genre fanbase to *MEKA*.

## 6. PRODUCTION NOTES / BTS HIGHLIGHTS

* **Authentic Psychological Soundscape:** The auditory landscape of *MEKA* was engineered in close collaboration with Dallas-based Deaf Mule Recording Studio to simulate the disorienting, binaural experience of auditory hallucinations, panic-induced tinnitus, and sonic claustrophobia.

* **Elevated Genre Comps:** *MEKA* strategically bridges the gap between auteur psychological horror (*Saint Maud*, *Relic*, *They Look Like People*) and commercial high-tension home invasion thrillers (*The Invisible Man*, *Hush*).
